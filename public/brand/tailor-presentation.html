The brief

A CV tailoring app helping job seekers shape their experience for each role. The identity should feel considered, personal and polished, with an editorial touch.

consideredpersonalpolishededitorial

The Stitched T should work as a one-colour symbol, stay recognizable in app and browser icons, and pair with a refined Tailor wordmark.

Final design

Stitched T

A T finished with precise stitch holes, expressing care in tailoring a CV to a role.

  • Simple letterform reads quickly as an app symbol
  • Stitches connect the Tailor name to careful, personal editing
  • Berry and editorial serif bring warmth to a job-search tool
